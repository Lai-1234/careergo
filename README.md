# CareerGo

**Live demo:** https://lai-1234.github.io/careergo/

Most career tools are built for one moment — graduating, job-hunting, hiring. CareerGo is built to stay with you for all of it.

CareerGo is a **Career Operating System** for anyone between the ages of 15 and 60 — from a student figuring out what to study, to someone starting their first job, to someone navigating a mid-career pivot or a senior leadership move. Instead of a one-time job board, it's a place you keep coming back to: understanding what to do next, growing the right skills, knowing what you're worth, tracking every application, and — on the other side — hiring the right people. All of it guided by an AI coach, Vera, who stays with you across the whole journey.

## Try it

Open the [live demo](https://lai-1234.github.io/careergo/) — nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

- Click **"Enter demo dashboard"** on any locked page, or submit the login form with the fields left blank, to open a fully set-up **job seeker** workspace.
- Choose **Employer** from the homepage to open a fully set-up **hiring** workspace the same way.
- Or click **Create Account** and go through sign-up yourself — every field is pre-filled with realistic defaults, so you can move through the whole thing without typing anything.

## What's inside

### For job seekers, at any stage of their career
- **Dashboard** — a daily overview that adapts to where you are and what you're working toward
- **Discover** — roles, companies, and universities matched to you, with a clear reason behind every match
- **Growth** — a personal skill roadmap with real, trackable progress
- **Career Value** — a live estimate of what you should be earning, with market comparisons and a negotiation simulator
- **Pipeline** — every application tracked from saved to offer, with automated matching working in the background
- **Feed** — a professional space to share milestones, ask questions, and connect with others
- **Vera** — an AI career coach woven into every part of the app, who remembers your goals and your history

### For employers
- A complete hiring workspace, entirely separate from the job seeker side
- A guided job-posting flow that checks salary competitiveness and flags biased language as you write
- A visual hiring pipeline you can move candidates through, with a match score for every applicant
- A company profile with real hiring analytics — funnel performance, pay compared to market, and an AI-generated health score
- The same Vera, reframed around hiring priorities instead of job searching
